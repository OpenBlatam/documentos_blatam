<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Achieve 100% records compliance", "Classify 50,000 records by retention schedule", "Dispose of 10,000 expired records annually")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, records-specific, and executable (e.g., "Deploy records management system with retention schedules", "Establish records classification framework categorizing by type and retention", "Implement automated disposal process for expired records")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Records team roles (e.g., Records Manager, Compliance Specialist, Classification Analyst, Disposal Coordinator)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Records management systems, Classification tools, Retention tracking, Archive systems)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $350K records technology, $150K compliance, $100K disposal)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Records management platform, Classification framework, Retention tracking system, Archive infrastructure)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague records terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
