| Objective | Strategy | Timeline | Resources |

No text after.

</formatting_protocol>

<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Track 100% of assets in real-time", "Achieve 99% inventory accuracy", "Improve asset utilization by 30%")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, asset tracking-specific, and executable (e.g., "Deploy asset tracking system with RFID tags for 1000 assets", "Implement barcode scanning for inventory management", "Establish asset lifecycle tracking from procurement to disposal")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Asset tracking team roles (e.g., Asset Manager, Inventory Specialist, Tracking Coordinator, Lifecycle Manager)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Asset tracking systems, RFID readers, Barcode scanners, Mobile apps)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $300K tracking technology, $150K tags and hardware, $100K implementation)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Asset tracking platform, RFID infrastructure, Mobile scanning system, Asset dashboard)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague asset tracking terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
