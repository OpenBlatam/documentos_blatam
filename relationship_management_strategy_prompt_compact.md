<formatting_protocol>

| Objective | Strategy | Timeline | Resources |

No text before.

No text after.

</formatting_protocol>

<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Build relationships with 100 key contacts", "Achieve 90% relationship satisfaction", "Increase relationship value by 40%")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, relationship-specific, and executable (e.g., "Deploy CRM system tracking 500 relationships with engagement history", "Establish relationship engagement program with monthly touchpoints", "Implement personalization engine customizing interactions based on preferences")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Relationship team roles (e.g., Relationship Manager, Engagement Specialist, Communication Coordinator, CRM Administrator)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., CRM systems, Communication platforms, Engagement tools, Analytics dashboards)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $250K relationship technology, $120K engagement, $80K communication)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., CRM platform, Engagement framework, Communication system, Relationship analytics dashboard)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague relationship terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
