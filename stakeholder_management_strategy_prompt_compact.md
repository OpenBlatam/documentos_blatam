<formatting_protocol>

You must deliver results ONLY in the following format:

| Objective | Strategy | Timeline | Resources |

No text before.

No text after.

Each row must represent a full stakeholder management execution line.

</formatting_protocol>

<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Achieve 90% stakeholder satisfaction", "Engage 50 key stakeholders monthly", "Build relationships with 20 strategic stakeholders")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, stakeholder-specific, and executable (e.g., "Map stakeholder landscape identifying 100 key stakeholders with influence analysis", "Deploy stakeholder engagement program with quarterly meetings and updates", "Establish communication framework with tailored messaging for each stakeholder group")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Stakeholder team roles (e.g., Stakeholder Manager, Relationship Manager, Communication Specialist, Engagement Coordinator)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Stakeholder management systems, CRM platforms, Communication tools, Survey platforms)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $200K stakeholder technology, $120K engagement, $80K communication)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Stakeholder management platform, Relationship tracking system, Communication framework, Engagement dashboard)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague stakeholder terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
