<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Achieve 100% regulatory compliance", "Obtain 5 product approvals in 18 months", "Reduce regulatory submission time by 40%")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, regulatory-specific, and executable (e.g., "Establish regulatory tracking system monitoring requirements across 10 jurisdictions", "Deploy submission management process streamlining approval workflows", "Implement regulatory intelligence program tracking changes and updates")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Regulatory team roles (e.g., Regulatory Affairs Manager, Compliance Specialist, Submission Coordinator, Regulatory Analyst)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Regulatory management systems, Compliance tracking tools, Submission platforms, Regulatory intelligence)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $300K regulatory technology, $200K compliance, $150K submissions)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Regulatory tracking system, Compliance management framework, Submission workflow, Regulatory intelligence platform)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague regulatory terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
