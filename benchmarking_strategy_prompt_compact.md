<formatting_protocol>

You must deliver results ONLY in the following format:

A Markdown table with EXACTLY 4 columns:

| Objective | Strategy | Timeline | Resources |

No text before.

No text after.

Each row must represent a full benchmarking execution line.

</formatting_protocol>

<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Benchmark 20 key performance metrics", "Compare performance against 10 competitors", "Identify 15 improvement opportunities")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, benchmarking-specific, and executable (e.g., "Conduct comprehensive benchmarking study comparing 30 metrics across 5 competitors", "Deploy competitive intelligence system tracking competitor performance", "Establish benchmarking framework with quarterly updates and analysis")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Benchmarking team roles (e.g., Benchmarking Manager, Competitive Analyst, Performance Analyst, Research Specialist)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Benchmarking platforms, Competitive intelligence tools, Analytics software, Research databases)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $250K benchmarking research, $120K tools, $80K analysis)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Benchmarking framework, Competitive intelligence system, Performance comparison dashboard, Research database)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague benchmarking terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
