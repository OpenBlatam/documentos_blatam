<formatting_protocol>

You must deliver results ONLY in the following format:

A Markdown table with EXACTLY 4 columns:

| Objective | Strategy | Timeline | Resources |

No text before.

No text after.

Each row must represent a full business development execution line.

</formatting_protocol>

<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Generate $5M in new business opportunities", "Establish 15 strategic partnerships", "Close 20 deals in Q1")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, BD-specific, and executable (e.g., "Implement lead generation program targeting 500 qualified prospects monthly", "Deploy CRM system tracking 200 active opportunities with pipeline management", "Establish strategic partnership program with 10 key industry players")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - BD team roles (e.g., Business Development Manager, Partnership Manager, Lead Generation Specialist, BD Analyst)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., CRM systems, Lead generation tools, Networking platforms, Analytics dashboards)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $300K BD activities, $150K tools, $100K events and networking)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., CRM platform, Lead tracking system, Partnership management framework, Opportunity pipeline dashboard)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague BD terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
