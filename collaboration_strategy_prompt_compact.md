<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Improve team collaboration score by 40%", "Reduce meeting time by 30%", "Achieve 90% tool adoption")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, collaboration-specific, and executable (e.g., "Deploy collaboration platform with chat, video, and document sharing", "Establish team workspaces for 20 project teams", "Implement collaboration best practices with training and guidelines")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Collaboration team roles (e.g., Collaboration Manager, Workplace Specialist, Communication Coordinator, Adoption Specialist)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Collaboration platforms, Video conferencing, Document sharing, Project management tools)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $350K collaboration technology, $150K training, $100K adoption)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Collaboration platform, Communication infrastructure, Workspace framework, Adoption dashboard)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague collaboration terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
