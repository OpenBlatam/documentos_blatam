<table_generation_rules>

- Each Strategy must be actionable, e-commerce-specific, and executable (e.g., "Optimize checkout process reducing cart abandonment by 30%", "Deploy personalization engine customizing product recommendations increasing AOV by 25%", "Implement mobile-first design improving mobile conversion rate by 40%")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - E-commerce team roles (e.g., E-commerce Manager, UX Designer, Conversion Specialist, Digital Marketing Manager)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., E-commerce platforms, Analytics tools, Personalization engines, Payment systems)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $300K platform, $200K marketing, $150K optimization)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., E-commerce platform, Payment processing, Analytics dashboard, Personalization system)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague e-commerce terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount
