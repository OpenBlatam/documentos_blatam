<formatting_protocol>

You must deliver results ONLY in the following format:

A Markdown table with EXACTLY 4 columns:

| Objective | Strategy | Timeline | Resources |

No text before.

No text after.

Each row must represent a full crisis management execution line.

</formatting_protocol>

<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Respond to crises within 1 hour", "Reduce crisis impact by 60%", "Maintain 95% business continuity")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, crisis-specific, and executable (e.g., "Establish crisis response team with 24/7 on-call rotation", "Deploy crisis communication protocol with pre-approved messaging templates", "Implement business continuity plan with 4-hour recovery time objective")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Crisis team roles (e.g., Crisis Manager, Communication Specialist, Business Continuity Coordinator, Risk Analyst)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Crisis management systems, Communication platforms, Monitoring tools, Business continuity software)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $300K crisis infrastructure, $150K training, $100K communication)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Crisis response framework, Communication system, Monitoring infrastructure, Business continuity plan)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague crisis terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
