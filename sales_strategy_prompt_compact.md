<formatting_protocol>

| Objective | Strategy | Timeline | Resources |

No text after.

</formatting_protocol>

<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Close $2M in new revenue Q1", "Increase average deal size by 30%", "Reduce sales cycle from 90 to 60 days")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, process-specific, and executable (e.g., "Implement MEDDIC qualification framework across all enterprise opportunities", "Launch outbound SDR program targeting 500 qualified leads monthly", "Deploy sales enablement playbooks for top 3 customer segments")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Sales team roles (e.g., Account Executive, SDR, Sales Manager, Sales Operations)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Salesforce, Outreach.io, Gong, LinkedIn Sales Navigator)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $100K sales tools, $50K training, $200K commission structure)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., CRM configuration, Sales automation, Lead scoring system, Sales analytics dashboard)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague sales terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
