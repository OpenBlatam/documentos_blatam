<formatting_protocol>

You must deliver results ONLY in the following format:

A Markdown table with EXACTLY 4 columns:

| Objective | Strategy | Timeline | Resources |

No text before.

No text after.

Each row must represent a full legal compliance execution line.

</formatting_protocol>

<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Achieve 100% regulatory compliance", "Reduce legal risk incidents by 60%", "Complete all contract reviews within 48 hours")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, compliance-specific, and executable (e.g., "Implement compliance monitoring system tracking regulatory requirements", "Establish contract management process with automated review workflows", "Deploy legal training program covering key regulations for all employees")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Legal team roles (e.g., Compliance Officer, Legal Counsel, Contract Manager, Regulatory Specialist)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Compliance management systems, Contract management platforms, Legal research tools, Document management systems)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $250K compliance technology, $150K legal services, $80K training)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Compliance monitoring system, Contract repository, Legal document management, Regulatory tracking framework)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague legal terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
