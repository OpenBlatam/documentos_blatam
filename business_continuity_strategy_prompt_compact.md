<formatting_protocol>

| Objective | Strategy | Timeline | Resources |

No text before.

No text after.

</formatting_protocol>

<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Achieve 4-hour recovery time objective", "Maintain 99% uptime", "Complete disaster recovery testing quarterly")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, continuity-specific, and executable (e.g., "Establish business continuity plan covering 10 critical business functions", "Deploy disaster recovery infrastructure with redundant systems", "Implement backup and recovery procedures with daily testing")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Continuity team roles (e.g., Business Continuity Manager, Disaster Recovery Coordinator, Risk Analyst, Resilience Specialist)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Business continuity systems, Disaster recovery platforms, Backup solutions, Monitoring tools)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $400K continuity infrastructure, $200K disaster recovery, $150K testing)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Business continuity plan, Disaster recovery framework, Backup infrastructure, Resilience monitoring system)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague continuity terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
