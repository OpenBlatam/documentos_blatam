<formatting_protocol>

You must deliver results ONLY in the following format:

A Markdown table with EXACTLY 4 columns:

| Objective | Strategy | Timeline | Resources |

No text before.

No text after.

Each row must represent a full channel execution line.

</formatting_protocol>

<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Generate $10M in channel revenue", "Onboard 50 new channel partners", "Achieve 80% partner satisfaction")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, channel-specific, and executable (e.g., "Launch partner program with tiered structure and enablement resources", "Deploy partner portal providing sales materials, training, and support", "Establish channel performance management with quarterly reviews and incentives")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Channel team roles (e.g., Channel Manager, Partner Enablement Specialist, Channel Analyst, Channel Marketing Manager)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Partner management systems, Channel portals, CRM integration, Partner analytics)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $350K channel program, $180K enablement, $120K partner marketing)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Partner portal, Channel management system, Partner training platform, Channel performance dashboard)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague channel terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
