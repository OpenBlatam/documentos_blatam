- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., IAM platforms, SSO solutions, Access management systems, Audit tools)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $400K access technology, $200K identity management, $150K compliance)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., IAM platform, SSO infrastructure, Access control framework, Audit system)

Avoid vague access terms.
