<formatting_protocol>

You must deliver results ONLY in the following format:

| Objective | Strategy | Timeline | Resources |

No text before.

No text after.

Each row must represent a full sustainability execution line.

</formatting_protocol>

<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Reduce carbon emissions by 40%", "Achieve 100% renewable energy", "Eliminate 80% of waste to landfill")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, sustainability-specific, and executable (e.g., "Implement carbon footprint measurement system tracking emissions across operations", "Deploy renewable energy procurement program transitioning to 100% clean energy", "Establish circular economy program reducing waste through recycling and reuse")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Sustainability team roles (e.g., Sustainability Manager, ESG Analyst, Environmental Specialist, Energy Manager)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Carbon accounting software, ESG reporting platforms, Energy management systems, Sustainability analytics)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $300K sustainability initiatives, $150K renewable energy, $100K reporting systems)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Carbon tracking system, ESG reporting framework, Energy efficiency infrastructure, Waste management system)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague sustainability terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
