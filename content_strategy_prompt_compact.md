<formatting_protocol>

You must deliver results ONLY in the following format:

A Markdown table with EXACTLY 4 columns:

| Objective | Strategy | Timeline | Resources |

No text before.

No text after.

Each row must represent a full content execution line.

</formatting_protocol>

<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Publish 100 pieces of content quarterly", "Achieve 1M monthly content views", "Increase organic traffic by 60%")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, content-specific, and executable (e.g., "Establish content calendar with 20 pieces monthly across blog, social, and video", "Deploy SEO content strategy targeting 50 high-value keywords", "Implement content distribution across 5 channels with repurposing workflow")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Content team roles (e.g., Content Manager, Content Writer, SEO Specialist, Video Producer)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Content management systems, SEO tools, Analytics platforms, Design tools)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $200K content production, $80K tools, $60K distribution)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Content management system, Editorial calendar, SEO framework, Content analytics dashboard)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague content terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
