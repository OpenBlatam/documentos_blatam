<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Generate $5M in partner-sourced revenue", "Establish 20 strategic partnerships", "Achieve 80% partner satisfaction score")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, partnership-specific, and executable (e.g., "Launch partner program with tiered structure and enablement resources", "Deploy partner portal providing sales materials and training", "Establish joint go-to-market strategy with 5 key partners")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Partnership team roles (e.g., Partnership Manager, Channel Manager, Alliance Director, Partner Enablement Specialist)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Partner management systems, Channel portals, CRM integration, Partner analytics)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $300K partner program, $150K enablement, $100K co-marketing)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Partner portal, Channel management system, Partner training platform, Alliance tracking framework)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague partnership terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
