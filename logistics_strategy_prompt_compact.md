<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Reduce logistics costs by 25%", "Achieve 98% on-time delivery", "Optimize routes reducing transportation time by 30%")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, logistics-specific, and executable (e.g., "Implement route optimization software reducing miles traveled by 20%", "Deploy transportation management system tracking shipments in real-time", "Establish carrier management program with 10 strategic partners reducing costs by 15%")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Logistics team roles (e.g., Logistics Manager, Transportation Coordinator, Route Planner, Carrier Manager)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Transportation management systems, Route optimization software, Tracking platforms, Carrier management tools)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $400K logistics technology, $250K transportation, $150K network optimization)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Transportation management system, Route optimization framework, Tracking infrastructure, Carrier network)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague logistics terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
