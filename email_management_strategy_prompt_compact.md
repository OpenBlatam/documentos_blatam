<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Reduce email processing time by 50%", "Achieve 100% email compliance", "Organize 1M emails with automated archiving")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, email-specific, and executable (e.g., "Deploy email management system with automated organization and archiving", "Implement email security policies with encryption and filtering", "Establish email retention policies with automated deletion")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Email team roles (e.g., Email Administrator, Communication Manager, Security Specialist, Archive Coordinator)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Email management systems, Security platforms, Archive systems, Compliance tools)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $300K email technology, $150K security, $100K archiving)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Email management platform, Security framework, Archive infrastructure, Compliance system)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague email terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
