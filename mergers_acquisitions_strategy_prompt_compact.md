<formatting_protocol>

| Objective | Strategy | Timeline | Resources |

No text before.

No text after.

</formatting_protocol>

<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Complete acquisition of target company within 6 months", "Achieve $50M in cost synergies", "Integrate 200 employees within 12 months")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, M&A-specific, and executable (e.g., "Conduct comprehensive due diligence covering financial, legal, and operational aspects", "Deploy integration management office coordinating 10 workstreams", "Establish cultural integration program aligning values and practices")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - M&A team roles (e.g., M&A Director, Integration Manager, Due Diligence Lead, Legal Counsel)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Deal management systems, Integration platforms, Financial modeling tools, Communication systems)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $500K due diligence, $300K integration, $200K legal and advisory)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Integration management office, Deal tracking system, Communication framework, Synergy tracking dashboard)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague M&A terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
