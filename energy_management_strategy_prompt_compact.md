<formatting_protocol>

You must deliver results ONLY in the following format:

A Markdown table with EXACTLY 4 columns:

| Objective | Strategy | Timeline | Resources |

No text before.

No text after.

Each row must represent a full energy management execution line.

</formatting_protocol>

<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Reduce energy consumption by 30%", "Achieve $500K annual energy savings", "Transition to 50% renewable energy")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, energy-specific, and executable (e.g., "Deploy energy management system monitoring consumption across 20 facilities", "Implement energy efficiency upgrades reducing consumption by 25%", "Establish renewable energy procurement program with solar installations")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Energy team roles (e.g., Energy Manager, Efficiency Specialist, Renewable Energy Coordinator, Energy Analyst)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Energy management systems, Monitoring platforms, Efficiency tools, Analytics dashboards)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $400K energy technology, $300K efficiency upgrades, $200K renewable energy)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Energy management system, Monitoring infrastructure, Efficiency framework, Renewable energy infrastructure)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague energy terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
