<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Launch 3 breakthrough products in 18 months", "File 15 patents in core technology areas", "Achieve 30% revenue from new products")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, innovation-specific, and executable (e.g., "Establish innovation lab with 5 dedicated researchers", "Implement stage-gate process managing 10 active R&D projects", "Deploy rapid prototyping framework reducing concept-to-prototype time by 50%")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Innovation team roles (e.g., Research Scientist, Innovation Manager, Product Innovator, IP Specialist)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., R&D software, Prototyping tools, Patent management systems, Innovation platforms)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $500K R&D, $150K prototyping, $100K IP protection)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Innovation lab, Prototyping facility, IP management system, Innovation portfolio framework)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague innovation terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
