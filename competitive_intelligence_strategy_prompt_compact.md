<formatting_protocol>

You must deliver results ONLY in the following format:

A Markdown table with EXACTLY 4 columns:

| Objective | Strategy | Timeline | Resources |

No text before.

No text after.

Each row must represent a full competitive intelligence execution line.

</formatting_protocol>

<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Monitor 15 key competitors", "Generate 50 competitive insights quarterly", "Identify 10 strategic opportunities")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, competitive-specific, and executable (e.g., "Deploy competitive intelligence platform tracking 20 competitors across 5 dimensions", "Establish competitive monitoring program with weekly updates", "Conduct competitive analysis covering pricing, features, and positioning")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Competitive team roles (e.g., Competitive Intelligence Manager, Market Analyst, Research Specialist, Intelligence Coordinator)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Competitive intelligence platforms, Market research tools, Analytics software, Monitoring systems)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $300K competitive technology, $150K research, $100K analysis)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Competitive intelligence platform, Monitoring framework, Analysis dashboard, Research database)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague competitive terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
