<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Achieve 85% resource utilization", "Improve demand forecast accuracy to 90%", "Reduce capacity costs by 25%")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, capacity-specific, and executable (e.g., "Implement capacity planning system forecasting demand 12 months ahead", "Deploy resource utilization tracking monitoring 200 resources", "Establish capacity optimization process balancing supply and demand")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Capacity team roles (e.g., Capacity Planner, Resource Manager, Demand Analyst, Optimization Specialist)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Capacity planning systems, Forecasting tools, Resource management platforms, Analytics dashboards)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $300K capacity technology, $150K forecasting, $100K optimization)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Capacity planning framework, Demand forecasting system, Resource tracking platform, Optimization dashboard)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague capacity terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
