<formatting_protocol>

You must deliver results ONLY in the following format:

A Markdown table with EXACTLY 4 columns:

| Objective | Strategy | Timeline | Resources |

No text before.

No text after.

Each row must represent a full asset management execution line.

</formatting_protocol>

<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Improve portfolio ROI by 20%", "Optimize asset allocation across 5 categories", "Reduce asset maintenance costs by 25%")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, asset-specific, and executable (e.g., "Implement asset tracking system monitoring performance of 200 assets", "Deploy portfolio optimization framework rebalancing allocation quarterly", "Establish asset lifecycle management process extending useful life by 30%")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Asset management team roles (e.g., Asset Manager, Portfolio Analyst, Asset Coordinator, Risk Manager)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Asset management systems, Portfolio analytics, Risk management tools, Performance dashboards)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $350K asset technology, $200K portfolio optimization, $150K maintenance)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Asset management system, Portfolio tracking framework, Performance analytics dashboard, Risk management system)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague asset management terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
