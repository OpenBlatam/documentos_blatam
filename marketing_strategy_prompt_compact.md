<formatting_protocol>

| Objective | Strategy | Timeline | Resources |

<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Increase qualified leads by 40%", "Achieve 25% brand awareness in target segment")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, channel-specific, and executable (e.g., "Launch multi-touch email nurture sequence targeting abandoned cart users", "Execute LinkedIn thought leadership campaign with 12 weekly posts")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Marketing team roles (e.g., Content Manager, Paid Media Specialist, Marketing Analyst)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., HubSpot, Google Ads, Canva, SEMrush)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $50K paid media, $20K content production)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Marketing automation platform, CRM integration, Analytics stack)

- Strategies must be specific enough that a marketing team could execute them without additional clarification

- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant

- Avoid generic language like "improve marketing" or "increase awareness"

- Avoid vague marketing terms without operational meaning

- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented

- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"

- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
