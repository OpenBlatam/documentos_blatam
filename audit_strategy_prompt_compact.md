<formatting_protocol>

You must deliver results ONLY in the following format:

A Markdown table with EXACTLY 4 columns:

| Objective | Strategy | Timeline | Resources |

No text before.

No text after.

Each row must represent a full audit execution line.

</formatting_protocol>

<table_generation_rules>

- Minimum of 5 rows, but aim for 8–12 if complexity justifies it

- Each Objective must be outcome-driven and measurable (e.g., "Complete 20 audits annually", "Achieve 100% compliance coverage", "Identify and remediate 50 audit findings")

- Each Strategy must be actionable, audit-specific, and executable (e.g., "Establish audit program covering 15 key areas with annual schedule", "Deploy audit management system tracking findings and remediation", "Implement continuous auditing for high-risk areas with quarterly reviews")

- Each Timeline must include real deadlines (days, weeks, quarters, or months)

- Each Resources cell must include:

  - Audit team roles (e.g., Audit Manager, Internal Auditor, Compliance Auditor, Risk Analyst)

  - Tools/platforms (e.g., Audit management systems, Compliance tracking tools, Risk assessment software, Documentation platforms)

  - Budget allocation (e.g., $300K audit technology, $200K audit services, $100K training)

  - Systems or infrastructure (e.g., Audit management framework, Compliance tracking system, Risk assessment framework, Audit reporting dashboard)

Avoid generic language.

Avoid vague audit terms.

- Strategies must be specific enough that a team could execute them without additional clarification
- Include expected KPIs or success metrics within the Strategy column when relevant
- Avoid generic language like "improve" or "increase" without specifics
- Avoid vague terms without operational meaning
- Everything must be concrete and execution-oriented
- If budget is unknown, use placeholder format: "[Budget TBD]" or "[Allocate based on priority]"
- If team size is unknown, specify roles needed rather than exact headcount

</table_generation_rules>
